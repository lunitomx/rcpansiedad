# Safety and privacy reference

Version: `0.1.0-alpha`

This is a product boundary, not a clinical protocol.

## Route before RCP

The only safe starting point is a clear indication that the person is not in immediate danger, is not thinking about harming themselves or someone else, can keep themselves safe and is not experiencing an urgent medical situation. Any yes, uncertainty or inability to answer reliably routes to human help.

The Skill must not decide that chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness or fear of death is “just anxiety.” It asks a short clarifying question and routes new, unexplained, severe, persistent or worsening symptoms to local emergency care. It does not diagnose panic attacks or heart conditions.

## Crisis response

Use the person's country only if the host can provide a current, verified resource. Otherwise say: contact your local emergency number or the nearest emergency department, and tell a trusted person who is nearby. Do not invent a hotline, promise a response from this project or ask the person to finish RCP first.

## Medical and medication boundary

Do not recommend starting, stopping, combining or changing medication. Do not interpret bodily symptoms, name a condition or advise waiting out a potentially urgent symptom. Encourage a qualified health professional or urgent care when medical judgment is needed.

## Data boundary

The intended path is:

`person's host → explicit local save → person's export/delete control`

It must never be:

`person → RCP project → maintainer dashboard`

No account, backend, analytics or network call is needed to complete RCP. The project does not receive journal entries, names or free-text analytics.
