# RCP flow reference

The digital adaptation preserves the five-part exercise while allowing text entry instead of handwriting. Writing by hand may be recommended, but it is not a condition for access.

1. **Stressful event** — briefly describe what happened, when and where.
2. **Emotions** — name emotions and rate their present intensity from 0 to 100.
3. **Automatic thoughts** — write the inner sentence as it appeared and rate belief from 0 to 100.
4. **Reconstruction** — write a more objective, caring and evidence-aware version in the person's own words.
5. **Read and reassess** — read the reconstruction if comfortable and rate the same emotions and thoughts again.

Facilitation rules:

- Do not combine steps unless the person asks for a shorter version.
- Do not fill in missing fields or convert a feeling into a fact.
- Ratings are personal snapshots, not a mental-health score.
- More objective does not mean positive or cheerful.
- The person decides whether the new wording is accurate and respectful.
- If distress increases, stop and return to human-help guidance or end the session.
