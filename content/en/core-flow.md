# RCP de la Ansiedad — core flow

Status: `release_candidate`; English adaptation of the Fer-confirmed method for controlled testing.

This writing exercise helps you sort through a difficult moment without turning your experience into a diagnosis. You choose what to share, may stop at any time and may seek professional help.

## 1. Stressful event

Briefly describe what happened, when and where. Share only the details you want to use.

## 2. Emotions

Name the emotions present and give each a personal intensity from 0 to 100. This number is a snapshot, not a test or diagnosis.

## 3. Automatic thoughts

Write the sentence or inner dialogue that appeared, as you remember it. Then rate how strongly you believe it from 0 to 100. The guide will not complete it for you.

## 4. Thought reconstruction

In your own words, write a version that is more objective, caring and connected to what you can check. Ask: What do I know for sure? What is uncertain? What would I say to someone I care about?

More objective does not mean forced positivity. It may include uncertainty, pain, boundaries and the need to ask for help.

## 5. Read and reassess

If comfortable, read your reconstruction silently or aloud. Then rate the same emotions and thoughts again from 0 to 100. You do not need a lower number for the exercise to be valid.
